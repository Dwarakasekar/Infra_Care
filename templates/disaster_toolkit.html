🧾 Response Protocol Guidelines

Emergency Procedures

  • Pre-Disaster Preparation

    1. Conduct safety training for all workers

    2. Secure loose materials and equipment

    3. Identify safe zones on site

    4. Backup important project data

  • During Disaster Actions

    1. Move all personnel to designated safe zones

    2. Shut off utilities if safe to do so

    3. Monitor official emergency channels

    4. Account for all team members

  • Post-Disaster Recovery

    1. Conduct site safety assessment

    2. Document damage for insurance

    3. Prioritize structural repairs

    4. Implement salvage plan for materials
