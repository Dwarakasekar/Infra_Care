ABOUT IC AND OUR WAY

Infra care – application aims to achieve a connective gap between clients and construction organizers, then predicts perfect season time by using GIS for construction works according to their surroundings. Augmented reality (AR) enhances construction planning by overlaying digital information on real-world environments, allowing for better visualization of projects. Offering a comprehensive, intelligent solution that integrates real-time data, advanced analytics, and community involvement to drive truly sustainable construction practices.

It is designed to enhance sustainability in the construction domain by promoting eco-friendly building practices. The platform offers guidance on using renewable materials and energy-efficient construction techniques tailored to each project’s location and environmental impact. By tracking project timelines and providing sustainability growth ideas, Infra-Care helps organizations minimize their carbon footprint while optimizing resource use. It also provides real-time updates and recommendations for disaster preparedness, ensuring that construction practices are resilient and future-proof. Through collaboration and communication features, Infra-Care fosters a community-driven approach to sustainable development in construction.

PROBLEM WE HAVE FACED

Rapid increase in construction activities and urbanization poses a significant threat to natural resources and wildlife habitat. This unsustainable development trajectory could lead to irreversible environmental degradation, loss of biodiversity, and drastic climatic changes.

According to United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), "the increased construction activities and urbanization will increase waste which will eventually destroy natural resources and wild life habitats over 70% of land surface from now up to 2032. "Moreover, construction uses around half of natural resources that humans consume. Production and transport of building materials consumes 25 - 50 percent of all energy used (depending on the country considered).
